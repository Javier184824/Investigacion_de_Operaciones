\subsection*{Conclusions of the experiment}

By executing the three algorithms and comparing the results of the Dynamic Programming Algorithm between the two Greedy Algorithms,
the results show that the Basic Greedy Algorithm does not hit same optimal solutions as the Proportional Greedy Algorithm. But a 
pattern can be noted, the algorithms do not scale well when amount of items increase. By increasing the size of the knapsack, looks 
like both algorithms tend to hit less optimal solutions, but not as much by increasing the amount of items.
