\chapter*{George Dantzig}
\begin{itemize}
    \item George Bernard Dantzig (1914-2005) was an American mathematician and computer scientist.
    \item He is best known for his development of the Simplex algorithm, a method for solving linear programming problems.
    \item Dantzig's work in optimization and operations research has had a profound impact on various fields, including economics, engineering, and logistics.
    \item He made significant contributions to the theory of linear programming, integer programming, and network flow problems.
    \item Dantzig's work has been widely recognized and has earned him numerous awards and honors throughout his career.
\end{itemize}

\chapter*{The Simplex Algorithm}

\begin{itemize}
    \item It is a ``mechanical'' method that allows to find optimal solutions to linear programming problems.
    \item It was invented by George Dantzig in 1947.
    \item Uses matrix operations until it finds the optimal solution or determines that the problem is unbounded or infeasible.
    \item It is based in a geometric interpretation of the problem, where the feasible region is a convex polytope and the optimal 
    solution is found at one of its vertices.
\end{itemize}
